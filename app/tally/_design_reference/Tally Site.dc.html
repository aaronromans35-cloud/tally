About

Most people learn money the expensive way.

The first credit card, the first overdraft, the first paycheck that disappears — that's where financial education usually happens, and it costs real money. Tally moves that learning earlier, to the years when the stakes are five dollars instead of five thousand.

We didn't build a bank. You already have one. We built the layer that makes sense of it.
